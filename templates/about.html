Art Forms & Dataset

A curated collection of iconic art movements used to train and evaluate our deep learning models

Early Renaissance

Realism, perspective, and balanced compositions shaped the foundation of modern Western art in this era.

Fauvism

Unrestrained color, expressive brushwork, and a joyful sense of freedom — this is Fauvism.

Minimalism

Defined by geometric shapes, limited palettes, and a focus on space and silence.

Pop Art

Bright, bold, and ironic — Pop Art embraces pop culture and mass media in visually explosive ways.

Rococo

Whimsical, elegant, and delicate — Rococo captures the softness and splendor of 18th-century romanticism.
